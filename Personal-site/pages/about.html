About Video Photo

Photographer &
Videographer

I am an aspiring videographer and photographer based in Brooklyn, NY. I started my journey with photography in 2019 when I bought my first mirrorless camera, a Sony A7ii. I would go out on the weekend with my friends and just do random photoshoots with them.

Over the years, I've shifted my photography from portraits to landscapes and cars. In 2023, I bought a Fujifilm X-T3 which was good at both photo and video. That's when I started trying out video and loved it. Ever since, I've spent more time shooting video.

Now, I mostly go out to take videos and bring a film camera for photography. In my video and photo pages, you'll see my favorite and most recent work.
